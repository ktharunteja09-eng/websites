1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Vaibhav Grand website is an enterprise-grade culinary web application and
direct-to-consumer (D2C) online ordering platform. Designed for a landmark family
restaurant in Renigunta (Tirupati), the platform bridges digital branding with
commission-free direct revenue generation.

Key Business Objectives:
- Eliminate 20-30% aggregator commissions (Swiggy / Zomato).
- Provide customers with transparent price comparisons and direct discounts.
- Enforce strict delivery zone boundary validation (5.0 km road radius).
- Provide instant mobile UPI checkout (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) & COD.
- Direct automated WhatsApp order receipt dispatch to the kitchen counter.
- Give restaurant management a live, PIN-protected Admin CRM to manage daily stock,
  flash offers, and incoming orders with audio chime alerts.

3. HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & DATA FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform follows a resilient Multi-Tier Architecture:

[Client Browser]
       │
       ├─► Static SSG Pre-rendered HTML (Instant TTFB, 100% crawlable by Google)
       │
       ├─► Cart & Calculation Engine (src/utils/cartMath.ts)
       │
       ├─► Geolocation Verification (OpenRouteService API + Haversine Radial Fallback)
       │
       ├─► Pre-Checkout Inventory Lock & Pricing Validation (src/utils/supabaseClient.ts)
       │       │
       │       ▼
       │   [Supabase PostgreSQL Backend]
       │   - Table: menu_items (Stock, completed status, delivery eligibility)
       │   - Table: store_offers (Active discounts, free item thresholds, countdown)
       │   - Table: orders (Permanent record of all orders and delivery coords)
       │   - Table: admin_secrets (Hashed PIN credentials locked from browser)
       │
       ├─► Mobile UPI Intent Gateway (upi://pay deep-links / QR fallback)
       │
       ├─► Automated WhatsApp Cloud Dispatch (Structured kitchen ticket formatting)
       │
       └─► Owner Admin CRM (/admin) (Real-time polling, audio chime, stock toggle)

DUAL-LAYER RESILIENCE & OFFLINE FALLBACK:
If internet connectivity drops, or Supabase credentials are not configured, the
app automatically switches to bundled fallback data (src/data/restaurantData.ts,
photoMenuData.ts, and sessionStorage/localStorage caches). Browsing, cart math,
discounts, and WhatsApp ordering continue without interruption.

4. DATABASE & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defined in supabase_setup.sql:

1. Table: menu_items
   - Columns: id, name, category, price, aggregator_price, is_veg,
     direct_order_eligible, is_completed, last_reset_date.
   - Purpose: Real-time stock status and direct-delivery eligibility.

2. Table: store_offers
   - Columns: id, is_active, banner_text, discount_percent, min_order_amount,
     free_item_name, free_item_min_amount, coupon_code, coupon_flat_discount,
     starts_at, expires_at.
   - Purpose: Centralized promotional rules controlling banners & checkout math.

3. Table: orders
   - Columns: id, order_code, customer_name, customer_phone, order_type,
     distance_km, address_notes, maps_link, items_json, subtotal,
     discount_amount, final_total, payment_method, status, created_at.
   - Purpose: Permanent auditable trail of all direct orders.

4. Table: admin_secrets
   - Columns: id, passcode (default PIN: '1234').
   - Purpose: Private table protected from all public access.

ROW LEVEL SECURITY (RLS) & RPC SECURITY:
- Anonymous public users can ONLY read `menu_items` and `store_offers`.
- Anonymous public users can ONLY INSERT into `orders` (cannot read other orders).
- All sensitive management operations run via SECURITY DEFINER PostgreSQL RPCs:
  * verify_admin_pin(input_pin): Server-side PIN verification.
  * admin_get_orders(input_pin): Fetches order feed for authenticated counter staff.
  * admin_update_order_status(input_pin, target_order_id, new_status): Updates order.
  * admin_update_menu_item(input_pin, item_id, completed_val, eligible_val): Toggles stock.
  * admin_reset_all_stock(input_pin): Daily morning batch reset.
  * admin_update_offers(...): Updates active flash sales and countdown timers.
  * admin_change_pin(input_pin, new_pin): Secure PIN change procedure.

5. KEY FEATURES BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. DIRECT ONLINE ORDERING SYSTEM (src/components/DirectOrderModal.tsx)
   - Dual Modes: Doorstep Delivery (5km limit) & Self-Pickup Takeaway.
   - GPS Road Distance Verification:
     * Checks OpenRouteService driving API for exact driving distance.
     * Enforces a strict 4-second Promise.race timeout.
     * Falls back to Haversine straight-line distance (threshold 3.8 km = 5.0 km road).
     * Takeaway orders completely bypass GPS distance requirements.
   - Operating Hours Lock: Orders are restricted to operating hours (11:30 AM to 10:30 PM).
   - Dynamic Cart Math Engine (src/utils/cartMath.ts):
     * Dynamically calculates subtotal, applied percentage discounts, and free gifts.
     * Strips discounts automatically if items are removed below minimum threshold.
   - Pre-Checkout Inventory Validation:
     * Queries Supabase before submission to verify no dish was marked sold-out.
     * Aborts with a clear modal alert if any item is out of stock.
   - Dual Payment Options:
     * Mobile UPI Deep-Link: Launches Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm with pre-filled amount.
     * Dynamic UPI QR Code: High-resolution QR code for desktop screen scanning.
     * Cash on Delivery (COD): Supported for local customers.
   - Kitchen WhatsApp Dispatch:
     * Builds a structured bill with Order ID, customer address, Google Maps pin,
       itemized breakdown, discount, and payment mode sent to WhatsApp counter.

B. AGGREGATOR VS DIRECT PRICE COMPARISON ENGINE (src/components/PriceComparisonModal.tsx)
   - Displays real-time transparent comparisons against Swiggy and Zomato prices.
   - Highlights typical 20-25% aggregator inflation (commissions, surge, platform fees).
   - Illustrates customer savings of ₹100-₹250 per meal when ordering directly.

C. 280+ DISH CULINARY MENU (src/components/MenuSection.tsx & PhotoMenu.tsx)
   - Categorized by 30+ groups: Arabic Mandi, Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, Clay Oven Tandoor,
     Starters, Soups, Curries, Breads, Chinese, and Beverages/Coolers.
   - Dual Viewing Modes:
     1. Classic A La Carte list with dietary filters (Veg / Non-Veg) and instant search.
     2. Photo Menu showcasing high-resolution food imagery and portion notes.
   - Smart Variant Grouping (Single, Half, Full, Family Pack, Jumbo Platter).
   - Mobile-First Controls:
     * Floating quick-jump category button.
     * Slide-over bottom sheet category drawer.
     * Horizontally scrolling sticky category pills linked to scroll position.
     * Verified photo bestsellers carousel.

D. DYNAMIC FLASH PROMOTIONS & SCARCITY COUNTDOWN (src/components/FlashOfferBanner.tsx)
   - Sticky top alert banner with a real-time countdown timer.
   - Displays threshold progress (e.g. "Add ₹80 more to unlock Free Cool Drink!").

E. OWNER ADMIN CRM & KITCHEN PORTAL (/admin or #admin)

   - Tab 2 (Flash Offers):

   - Tab 3 (Live Orders CRM):
     * Auto-polls every 10 seconds for new orders.

F. INTERACTIVE CATERING & BANQUET PLANNER (src/components/CateringEvents.tsx)
